(Authoritative Version – Qatar Law Firms)

QATAR LAW FIRM MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
MASTER IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE FOR DEVELOPMENT TEAM

This document defines the ONLY accepted workflow.
It reflects real law firm operations in the State of Qatar.
No assumptions. No shortcuts. No generic CRM logic.




1. CORE PRINCIPLES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

• Payment may occur BEFORE case creation
• invoice 🧾 first to creat by secretary 
• Lawyers NEVER sign memorandums
• Managing Partner is the ONLY legal signer
• Approving Lawyer approval is mandatory before signature
• Not every session or stage requires a memorandum
• Some sessions require documents only
• No court session without official submission
• WhatsApp is the primary client communication channel
• Full audit trail is mandatory
• System must support 1 or 2 secretaries per office

⸻

2. USER ROLES & DASHBOARDS (STRICT SEPARATION)

A. Secretary (Front Desk / Operations)
	•	Collect payments (pre-case or per stage)
	•	Create payment record BEFORE case exists
	•	Open case ONLY after payment confirmation
	•	Upload POA and client documents
	•	Assign:
	•	Drafting Lawyer
	•	Approving Lawyer
	•	Record final decisions
	•	Close cases
	•	Archive cases
	•	Send WhatsApp messages (manual & automated)

❌ Cannot draft
❌ Cannot approve legally
❌ Cannot sign

⸻

B. Drafting Lawyer
	•	Draft memorandums (when required)
	•	Upload supporting documents
	•	Edit drafts until approval
	•	Submit draft for approval

❌ Cannot approve
❌ Cannot sign
❌ Cannot submit to court

⸻

C. Approving Lawyer (Senior / Proving Lawyer)
	•	Review legal content
	•	Approve or reject memorandums
	•	Decide per session/stage:
	•	Documents only
	•	Memorandum required
	•	Both
	•	Request revisions

❌ Cannot sign
❌ Cannot submit

⸻

D. Managing Partner / Owner
	•	Review approved memorandums
	•	Apply the ONLY legal signature
	•	Lock signed documents permanently
	•	Reject and return for revision
	•	View analytics & reports

⸻

E. Legal Secretary (Court Handling)
	•	Submit signed documents to:
	•	Court portals
	•	Prosecution portals
	•	Enter:
	•	Reference number
	•	Submission date
	•	Upload official proof (PDF)

❌ Cannot submit unsigned documents

⸻

F. Accountant
	•	Payments
	•	Invoices
	•	Financial reports only

⸻

G. System (Automation Layer)
	•	Enforce workflow rules
	•	Lock/unlock stages
	•	Trigger WhatsApp reminders
	•	Notify employees
	•	Maintain audit logs
	•	Block illegal actions automatically

⸻

3. PAYMENT LOGIC (IMPORTANT CORRECTION)

❌ NO payment per memorandum by default

✅ Payment is:
	•	Per CASE STAGE
	•	Per SESSION
	•	Additional payment can be added anytime for the same case

Examples:
	•	First Instance stage → payment
	•	Appeal stage → new payment
	•	Enforcement → new payment
	•	Extra work → additional payment linked to stage/session

System rules:

IF payment_status != PAID
BLOCK next workflow step




4. CASE & SESSION LOGIC (REAL PRACTICE)

Sessions
	•	A case can have multiple sessions
	•	A session may:
	•	Be extended
	•	Be adjourned
	•	Require documents only
	•	Require a new memorandum

Memorandum Rules
	•	NOT every session requires a memorandum
	•	Approving Lawyer decides
	•	New memorandum ONLY if legally required
	•	Old memorandums remain read-only

⸻

5. END-TO-END WORKFLOW
	1.	Payment collected (even before case)
	2.	Case created
	3.	Stage selected
	4.	Drafting Lawyer prepares (if required)
	5.	Approving Lawyer approves
	6.	Managing Partner signs (if memo exists)
	7.	Legal Secretary submits to portal
	8.	Reference number recorded
	9.	System unlocks session
	10.	Session occurs / extended
	11.	Lawyer records outcome
	12.	Secretary records decision
	13.	Case closed
	14.	System archives (read-only)

⸻

6. JUDICIAL STAGES (QATAR)

Each stage generates a NEW reference number:
	•	Prosecution
	•	Investigation
	•	First Instance
	•	Appeal
	•	Cassation (Tamyeez)
	•	Enforcement

⸻

Client Notifications

Employee Notifications

8. REPORTING (MANDATORY)

Per Case
	•	Timeline
	•	Sessions
	•	Payments
	•	Decisions
	•	Documents
	•	WhatsApp history
	•	Audit log

Monthly / Annual

Export:

9. ARCHIVING

Conditions:
	•	Final decision recorded
	•	No open sessions
	•	Case closed

Archive:
	•	Permanent
	•	Read-only
	•	Full history preserved
